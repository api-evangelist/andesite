# Andesite

> Andesite is a cybersecurity company building a Human+AI Security Operations Center (SOC) platform that connects human analysts, AI agents, and security data to automate and accelerate threat detection, investigation, and response. It carries FedRAMP High Authorization and SOC 2 Type II compliance and is a General Catalyst portfolio company in the defense and government sector.
